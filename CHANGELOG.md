# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-05)

- 3,170 fictional bank transactions from 12 small businesses, April to September 2026, labelled against a 34-account chart with a difficulty and a reason for each label.
- Warm and cold dev/test splits.
- `cli/score.py`: accuracy, dollar-weighted accuracy, macro F1, material errors, deferrals, breakdowns and top confusions, as text, JSON or Markdown.
- Two merchant-lookup baselines with predictions for both splits.
- Tests, manifest, checksums and release zip.
